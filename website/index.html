One wallet for everything with a chip or a code

Add a card by holding it to your phone, pointing the camera at its barcode, opening a pass file, or typing it in.

Transit cards

Read Calypso cards like MOBIB to see the serial, contracts and trip counters, without ever writing to the card.

Boarding passes

Scan the barcode on any airline's boarding pass and the flight, route, seat and booking reference fill in by themselves.

Tickets and passes

Open Apple Wallet .pkpass files from mail or your browser. Upcoming ones move to the top of your wallet.

Loyalty and gift cards

Barcodes and QR codes shown big and at full brightness, so the checkout scanner gets them first time, even in a basement with no signal.

NFC tags and badges

Read any tag, clone NDEF tags, or build your own: links, text, Wi-Fi logins or a contact card your phone can hand over.

Yours to design

Pick colours and gradients, add your own artwork, choose what shows on the card face, and theme the whole app light, dark or pure black.

Your phone becomes the card

CardVault answers NFC readers itself using Android's host card emulation. It only does that for the card you unlocked, and only for as long as you chose.

  • Off until you say soA card responds only after a fresh fingerprint or PIN, for 15 seconds up to 2 minutes.
  • Tags and custom cardsEmulates NFC Forum Type 4 tags and your own ISO-DEP responses with any AID.
  • See every byteA live console shows each command from the reader and every answer your phone sends.

Built for the day you lose your phone

No account, no ads, no analytics. The app only goes online when you back up to your own Google Drive or check for updates.

What CardVault protects against and how
Someone picks up your unlocked phoneThe app and each card ask for your fingerprint, face or PIN. Card numbers stay masked until you choose to show them.
Skimming through your pocketNothing answers a reader unless you unlocked a card a moment ago, and Android also needs the phone itself to be unlocked.
Someone copies the phone's storageCard data is encrypted with AES-256 using a key locked inside the phone's security chip, in a database that is encrypted too.
Your Google account is breachedBackups are encrypted on the phone with your passphrase before upload. Without it, Google and we see only noise.
A fake updateAndroid installs updates only if they are signed with our release key, and the app checks the published checksum first.

What it won't do

Some cards are protected by hardware no app can copy. We'd rather tell you now than after you're stuck at a gate.

Replace your transit card at the gate

Gates check a signature from the card's secure chip. CardVault reads your card, but you still tap the real one.

Pay

Bank cards are detected and refused. Keep using your bank's wallet for that.

Copy hotel keys and old badges

MIFARE Classic and UID-only cards can't be emulated on Android. You can still store their details as a reference.

Tap loyalty cards

Hardly any shop reads loyalty cards over NFC, so CardVault shows their barcode instead.

Install in a minute

CardVault isn't on the Play Store. You install it straight from our releases, and it keeps itself up to date after that.

  1. Download the APK

    Get the latest release on your phone.

  2. Allow the install

    Open the file. Android asks once whether your browser may install apps; allow it.

  3. Stay up to date

    New versions appear in the app under Settings, Updates. One tap installs them.
